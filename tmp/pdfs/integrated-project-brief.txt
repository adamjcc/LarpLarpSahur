Two themes; choose one to do: 
 
THEMATIC CHOICE 1 
 
 
Delta Challenge 2026 Track B: Guardians of Tomorrow Problem Statements: 
General Delta Challenge2026 :is organised by National Crime Prevention Council and the Singapore 
Police Force. The Track B of the Delta Challenge has following 3 problem statements: 
1. Crime prevention,     
How to stop crimes from happening before they occur. It involves educating the public, 
strengthening security measures, building community partnerships, and encouraging vigilance 
among residents and businesses. 
2. Scam education,     
What novel and effective solutions can be implemented to prevent youths from falling prey to 
online scams? Propose a project to protect youths against scams. 
3. Road safety education.  
How can we create an engaging and interactive road safety education programme that 
resonates with today's youth? 
 
Requirement: To conceptualize, propose, create and develop a whole 3D Virtual Experience 
based off 1 out of 3  of Delta Challenge 2026 Track B: Guardians of Tomorrow Problem Statements. 
Project submissions close on 31 August 2026. 
• The team choose to do Delta challenge, and submit (show evidence to tutor) on week 17 will get 
3 extra marks across all 4 modules.

For 3RT - Game Assets, Lighting & Post Processing, VFX 
1. Modular assets for flexible spatial interior environment construction are required to be built for the 
project. 
2. Must create from scratch the assets required to build at least 1 entire room (meaning no 
Unity Asset Store, Sketchfab, or any online free 3D meshes.) 
3. Texture modular assets with texture atlas/trim sheet texturing method. 
4. Utilize proper lighting techniques (lightmap baking with mixed mode lights) 
5. Apply post processing techniques (not just Bloom, explore Color Toning, and Vignette also). 
6. Create appropriate VFX. 
 
3RT Guiding Factors & Tasks 
In consideration of 3 or 4 members (say A, B, & C [or C, & D]) 
1. Visual research work for reference before embarking on 3D modelling with PureRef (teamwork) 
2. 3D Modelling – spatial modular assets and set dressing prop modular assets by member A 
(Note: If there is a 4th member D, have member A do spatial modular assets, and member 
D do set dressing prop modular assets.) 
3. Member B to create texture atlas/trimsheet and texture modular assets. 
4. Trim sheet should be used on X3 (or X4 for 4-member team) or more assets (UV unwrap 
and layout work should be done by member B). 
5. Member C to create X3 (or X4 for 4-member team) VFX at least to be used in scene. 
6. Set up mixed mode lighting and bake lightmaps in Unity (teamwork) 
7. Apply post processing in Unity (teamwork) 
8. Naming convention for the file should be eg. 3RT_<NameOf
